Nandana Super Shop was founded with a straightforward mission: to provide Sri Lankan households with premium, long-lasting plastic goods and home essentials without inflated price tags.

Whether you are organizing a kitchen pantry with airtight food containers, furnishing a veranda with durable plastic chairs, or managing laundry with sturdy baskets, we stock carefully selected products tailored for practical everyday convenience.
